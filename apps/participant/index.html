How it works

You'll have a spoken conversation with an AI persona in a realistic workplace scenario. The persona will play a defined role/persona and respond dynamically based on the conversation.

    1
    Sign in
    Use the name, email and access code from your invitation.
    2
    Read the scenario
    You'll see who you'll be speaking with and what the conversation is about.
    3
    Run a quick setup check
    We check your microphone, speakers and connection, and your camera if it's needed.
    4
    Have the conversation
    Speak naturally, as you would in the real situation. There's no script.
    5
    Submit
    Your conversation is sent to the team that invited you.

For the best experience, use Chrome or Edge in a quiet place, with headphones if you have them.

What this is
  • A spoken conversation with an AI persona who plays a role in a realistic situation, such as a customer, a manager or a colleague.
  • There are no right or wrong scripts. Respond as you would in the real situation.
  • It takes about the time shown under "Before you start".
Before you start
  • Use a computer or phone with an up-to-date Chrome, Edge or Safari.
  • Find a quiet place, and use headphones with a microphone if you can.
  • Choose a time on the overview screen. You can join from 5 minutes before your time until 15 minutes after.
  • When your browser asks for the microphone, select Allow. The setup check confirms it works.
During the conversation
  • The persona speaks first. When it finishes, the status shows Your turn.
  • Speak naturally. A short pause to think is fine; the persona waits for you to finish.
  • You can ask the persona to repeat or rephrase anything.
  • The text under the orb shows what was heard from you, so you can check it was understood.
  • Use Mute if you need a moment, and End conversation when you are done.
If something goes wrong
  • Can't hear the persona: check your volume and output device. Replies also appear on screen.
  • The persona can't hear you: check you're not muted and that the right microphone is selected in your browser.
  • Disconnected: select Resume conversation. You rejoin at the same point, and everything so far is saved.
  • Refreshing the page is safe. Sign in again with the same access code and email to continue.
After you finish
  • Your conversation is submitted automatically when it ends.
  • Results go to the team that invited you. You will not see a score here.
  • A short feedback form helps improve the experience. It is optional.
Privacy
  • The conversation is recorded and transcribed only if you agreed when you signed in.
  • The persona is given only your name, so it can greet you. Nothing else about you is shared with it.
