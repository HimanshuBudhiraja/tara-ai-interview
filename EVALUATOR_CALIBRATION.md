# Evaluator calibration

Generated by `tools/write_calibration_report.py` from `evals/results/`. Every
number below comes from a run; nothing here is hand-edited.

The question this answers is not "does the evaluator run" — Phase 10 settled
that — but "are its decisions right". It is measured against
`evals/datasets/evaluator_benchmark.json`, whose expected outcomes are authored
to be ones a human reviewer would agree with, and whose criterion expectations
are **ranges**: inside the range is calibration variance, outside it is error.

## Headline

```
cases                              47
free of material error             43  (92%)
fully inside every authored range  40  (85%)
material errors                    4
calibration variances              8
```

Two rates on purpose. A single pass percentage would let "scored a skill nobody
asked about" hide inside "was one point low on Clarity", and those are not the
same kind of wrong. The first number is the one that gates anything.
